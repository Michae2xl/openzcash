Committee · how a decision is born

From an invitation to a signed decision

Five seats, one vote each, a public reason, and a record with a timestamp and a hash.

01

Invite

The Owner invites a verified email with a role. The invitee accepts with a public reviewer name and consent.

02

Verify email

Sign in with an email code. No password, no wallet. The seat stays "not ready" until the email is verified.

03

Passkey

The reviewer enrolls a passkey (WebAuthn) on their own device. Votes are signed with it.

04

Review opened

A Coordinator opens a review for one revision. The roster, the keys and the rules freeze for that round.

05

Verdicts

Each seat signs one choice, approve, reject or abstain, and a public reason. The choice cannot be changed.

06

Decision with timestamp

Computed only after all five verdicts. Recorded in the history as a signed entry with a hash.

Seats
5

One vote per member. Moderators manage discussions and never vote.

Approvals to pass
3of 5

60 percent of the committee. An abstention counts as a response, not as an approval.

Quorum
All 5

No deadline. The result is published only after every seat has recorded a choice.

Every decision

Timestamp and signed record

Access changes, rules, review openings and decisions enter the history with a sequence number and a hash. Receipts download as JSON and verify with the published script.
