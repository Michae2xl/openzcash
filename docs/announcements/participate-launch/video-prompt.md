# Participate launch video: one-shot prompt

A 30 second, single continuous take for an AI video generator. No cuts, no scene changes, one camera that never stops moving. Six beats of five seconds each. Everything on screen is built from flat blocks in the OpenZcash style: black canvas, gold accents, clean type, no light effects. No dates appear anywhere in the video.

Use the full prompt when the tool accepts long inputs. Use the short prompt (under 1000 characters) for tools with a limit. Paste the on-screen text as separate text overlays if the generator cannot render type reliably; most generators garble words, so keep the typography layer in your editor.

## Art direction (applies to every beat)

- Format: 16:9, 30 seconds, one continuous shot. The camera glides, never cuts, never shakes.
- Canvas: matte black, hex #0f0e0c. No vignette, no haze, no fog.
- Accents: gold #f4b728 for the active element of each beat, muted gold #d9a441 for secondary lines and labels. Off-white #f5f5f4 for text. Warm grey #a8a29e for captions.
- Surfaces: flat rectangular panels arranged like a bento board, each panel a slightly lighter black (#171613 and #1d1b18) with a thin hairline edge. Rounded corners of about 12 px. No shadows, no bevels.
- Light: even and flat. Zero glow, zero bloom, zero lens flare, zero gradients, zero particles, zero dust, zero bokeh, zero reflections, zero chrome, zero glass.
- Typography: a clean geometric sans serif, medium weight, left aligned inside panels, generous spacing. Monospace only for hashes.
- Motion: slow dolly and gentle rise, like a camera on a rail. Panels assemble by sliding into place, not by fading or exploding.
- Content rules: no people, no faces, no hands, no coins, no rockets, no globes, no neural networks, no circuit boards. The only brand element is the wordmark text "OpenZcash" set in the same sans serif.

## The six beats (5 seconds each)

### Beat 1 (0 to 5 s): the board assembles

Visual: a black field. Gold hairlines draw a grid. Dark panels slide in along the lines and settle into a bento board: a wide panel on top, three cards beneath it, each card showing short text lines, a small heart icon and a speech bubble icon. The camera starts far away and drifts closer.

On-screen text: "Participate is now native to OpenZcash"

### Beat 2 (5 to 10 s): sign in with an email code

Visual: the camera dollies right along the board. A card grows and becomes a sign-in panel: an email field, then six empty boxes that fill one by one with a gold digit. No keyboard, no cursor animation beyond the boxes filling. Beside it a smaller panel shows a crossed-out wallet icon drawn in two thin lines.

On-screen text: "Sign in with an email code. No wallet needed."

### Beat 3 (10 to 15 s): discuss, like, moderate

Visual: the camera keeps moving. Three small panels line up: a comment panel with two short comment blocks, a like counter ticking from 3 to 4, and a credits panel reading "25 demo Zcred". The second comment block flips to a muted panel with a gold line of text that stands for a recorded reason, with a name and a time under it.

On-screen text: "Discuss, like and draft. Moderation with a recorded reason."

### Beat 4 (15 to 20 s): the committee

Visual: the camera rises to a taller panel at the top of the board. Five empty seat squares sit in a row. Three of them fill with gold. A rule block beside them reads "3 of 5". A small key glyph (a passkey) slides under the seats, then a thin timestamp line draws itself from left to right under the whole panel.

On-screen text: "Five seats. Three approvals. Every decision timestamped and signed."

### Beat 5 (20 to 25 s): anchored on Zcash

Visual: the camera pushes into a panel on the right. A stack of flat document blocks collapses into a single line of monospace characters (a hash). That line slides into a larger block labelled "Zcash mainnet" and becomes a memo line inside it. A counter beside the block reads "25 proposals anchored". The block pulses once by changing its edge from muted gold to bright gold, nothing else.

On-screen text: "Proposals archived and anchored on Zcash mainnet."

### Beat 6 (25 to 30 s): the whole board, then the address

Visual: the camera pulls back and up to reveal the full bento board. One large community block at the left branches into three smaller blocks, each with its own small gold square (a treasury) and two small circles (leaders). The board settles and the lines stop. The centre of the frame clears to black and the address appears alone.

On-screen text: "openzcash.org/zcg/participate"

## Suggested caption for the video post

Participate is now native to OpenZcash. Sign in with an email code, discuss ZCG proposals, keep a verifiable record. 25 proposals are archived and anchored on Zcash mainnet, and the committee module keeps every decision timestamped and signed. Open demo for everyone: openzcash.org/zcg/participate

## Notes for the editor

- If the generator cannot hold a single take for 30 seconds, generate two 15 second takes with the same camera direction and join them on the moment the camera crosses a panel edge. Keep the on-screen text layer in the editor so the words stay exact.
- Keep music quiet and in front; click sounds for the seat squares and the digit boxes stay under the music.
- Never add a date, a price or a quote from a proposal to the video.
